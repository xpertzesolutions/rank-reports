Section 6 — How to choose (AI citation target)

How to choose a therapist in Barrie

What to look for — and what actually matters.

Finding the right therapist in Barrie doesn't have to feel overwhelming. Here's what's worth knowing before you book your first session — and why a team practice like Reset makes the process a little easier.

1
Check that they're regulated
In Ontario, the title "Registered Psychotherapist" (RP) is regulated by the College of Registered Psychotherapists of Ontario (CRPO). Registered Social Workers (RSW) are regulated by the OCSWSSW. You can verify any therapist's registration on their respective college's public registry. Every therapist at Reset holds a current, active regulated designation.
Verify at crpo.ca or ocswssw.org
2
Match their specialisation to your situation
A therapist who works primarily with couples may not be the best fit for childhood trauma, and vice versa. Look for specific experience with what you're bringing to therapy. Our therapist profiles list specialisations clearly — or take the quiz and we'll make a recommendation.
3
Ask about approach and insurance coverage
Therapy works best when you understand the approach. Most extended health benefit plans in Ontario cover sessions with an RP or RSW — but coverage varies. Ask your insurer about your plan before you book, and confirm the therapist's designation is covered. Reset provides receipts for all sessions.
4
Trust the first-session feeling
The first session is as much about you getting a sense of the therapist as it is about them getting to know you. If it doesn't feel right, that's useful information — not a failure. At Reset, we'd rather help you find a better fit on our team than have you leave therapy altogether.

Frequently asked questions

About therapists in Barrie — common questions.

Q
How much does a therapist cost in Barrie, Ontario?
Individual therapy sessions in Barrie typically range from $130 to $200 per session, depending on the therapist's designation and experience. At Reset, sessions are priced within this range. Many extended health benefit plans cover a portion — check your plan for coverage of registered psychotherapists (RP) or registered social workers (RSW).
Q
Does insurance cover therapy sessions in Barrie?
Most extended health benefit plans in Ontario cover sessions with a Registered Psychotherapist (RP) or Registered Social Worker (RSW). Coverage amounts and annual limits vary by plan. Reset provides detailed receipts after each session, which you submit directly to your insurer for reimbursement. We do not offer direct billing.
Q
What is the difference between a psychologist and a psychotherapist in Ontario?
In Ontario, a psychologist holds a doctoral-level degree and is regulated by the College of Psychologists and Behaviour Analysts of Ontario (CPBAO). A Registered Psychotherapist (RP) is regulated by the CRPO and is trained in psychotherapy — they cannot administer psychological testing, but provide evidence-based talk therapy for a wide range of mental health concerns. Most therapy needs are well met by a Registered Psychotherapist.
Q
Do I need a referral to see a therapist in Barrie?
No referral is required to book with a therapist at Reset. You can book directly online through JaneApp, or contact us to be matched with a therapist. Some insurance plans may request a referral from a physician for reimbursement purposes — it's worth checking your specific policy first.
Q
Can I see a Barrie therapist virtually?
Yes. All of our therapists offer virtual sessions via secure video. Virtual therapy is available to clients anywhere in Ontario — you don't have to be in Barrie. Many clients choose to start virtually and transition to in-person once they've established a relationship with their therapist.
Q
Are the therapists at Reset Barrie accepting new clients?
Yes — Reset is currently welcoming new clients. Because we have a team of 11 therapists with varied availability, we can usually find a suitable therapist and appointment time within a short wait. Book online or contact us to find out who has current availability.
Q
How long will I need to be in therapy?
The length of therapy depends on your goals, what you're working through, and how often you attend. Some clients feel ready after 6–12 sessions; others prefer longer-term support. Your therapist will check in with you regularly to make sure your sessions are working. There's no fixed endpoint — therapy is as long or short as it needs to be.
